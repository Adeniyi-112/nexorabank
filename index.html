Frequently asked questions

What is NEXORA BANK? +
NEXORA BANK is currently a proposed banking brand and website concept. It is not a licensed financial institution.

Nigerian Naira (NGN) and United States Dollar (USD) are the planned currencies. Availability depends on regulatory approval and financial partnerships.
Can I transfer money now? +
No. Money transfers and deposits are not currently available on this website.
Can I create a real bank account? +
Real account opening is not yet available. It will require an approved financial institution and compliant account-opening systems.
