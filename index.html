Frequently asked questions

Questions about NEXORA

Find answers to common questions about this platform.

What is NEXORA BANK? +
NEXORA BANK is a proposed digital banking website concept focused on a modern and accessible financial experience.
Can I open a real bank account? +
No. Real account opening is not available. It would require an appropriately licensed financial institution and the necessary regulatory approvals.
Can I deposit or transfer money? +
No. This website does not accept deposits, hold funds or process financial transfers.
Which currencies are featured? +
The concept currently highlights Nigerian Naira (NGN) and United States Dollar (USD).
When will banking services become available? +
No launch date has been announced. Financial services would only be introduced after the required partnerships and regulatory approvals.
